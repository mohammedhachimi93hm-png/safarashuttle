Two possible roads

Inland, the fastest: about 4 hr 30 min, ending on the argan tree road.

Along the coast, the prettiest: El Jadida, Oualidia and Safi, by the ocean, but longer. Tell us your choice when you book.

In Essaouira

The medina is pedestrian. The car stops at the car park by the walls, and porters with carts take you to your riad.

Staying outside the medina, on the beach or towards Diabat? Give the exact address when you book.

Good to know

  • Kitesurf gear or boards: take the van (3,000 MAD, up to 8 people).
  • Essaouira → Marrakech? Choose Essaouira as the pick-up in the booking form.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca to Essaouira?

2,300 MAD per car by sedan (1 to 3 passengers) and 3,000 MAD by van (up to 8 passengers). Fixed price, tolls included.

How long does it take?

About 4 hr 30 min for 360 km inland. Along the coast, allow longer.

Can we stop at an argan oil cooperative?

Yes, ask for a stop when you book.

How do I cancel?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your booking reference. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
