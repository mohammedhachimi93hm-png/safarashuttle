The ride

No need to cross Casablanca: from the airport we join the Marrakech motorway directly, via Berrechid and Settat.

A wide, straight road with service areas for a coffee stop. About 2 hr 40 min.

In Marrakech

Medina and Jemaa el-Fna, Gueliz, Hivernage, Palmeraie: one price for the whole city.

Is your riad inside the medina? The lanes are closed to cars: the driver drops you at the nearest gate and a porter with a cart walks you to the door.

Why not the train?

From the airport you first have to reach a Casablanca station, often change trains, then find a taxi in Marrakech.

By car it is one single ride, from the airport exit to your hotel door.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca airport to Marrakech?

1,800 MAD per car by sedan (1 to 3 passengers), 2,000 MAD by van (up to 8 passengers), 2,900 MAD by Mercedes E-Class. Fixed price, tolls included.

Is the price per person?

No, it is the price of the whole car with its driver, however many passengers.

How long does it take?

About 2 hr 40 min for 214 km of motorway.

And from Marrakech to Casablanca airport?

Same price. Allow 2 hr 40 min of driving plus check-in: leave Marrakech about 5 hours before an international flight.

Where do I meet the driver at the airport?

In the arrivals hall, just after customs, holding a sign with your name. You get the driver’s name and WhatsApp number the day before.

My flight is delayed. What happens?

We track your flight with the number you give us. Your driver comes at the real landing time and waits 60 minutes at no charge.
