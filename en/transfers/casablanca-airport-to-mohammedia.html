The ride

We skip central Casablanca: the motorway goes round the city to the east, then on to Mohammedia on the coast.

About 50 minutes, often quicker than reaching central Casablanca at rush hour.

In Mohammedia

The corniche and beaches, the golf course, the port, seafront hotels and business parks: give the exact address when you book and the driver drops you at the door.

Here for work? The driver can also stay with you: choose “Chauffeur by the hour” in the booking form.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca airport to Mohammedia?

500 MAD per car by sedan (1 to 3 passengers) and 600 MAD by van (up to 8 passengers). Fixed price, tolls included.

How long does it take?

About 50 minutes for 54 km.

Can you drive from Mohammedia to Rabat or Marrakech?

Yes. Choose Mohammedia as the pick-up in the booking form: the price of any route shows straight away.

Where do I meet the driver at the airport?

In the arrivals hall, just after customs, holding a sign with your name. You get the driver’s name and WhatsApp number the day before.

My flight is delayed. What happens?

We track your flight with the number you give us. Your driver comes at the real landing time and waits 60 minutes at no charge.
