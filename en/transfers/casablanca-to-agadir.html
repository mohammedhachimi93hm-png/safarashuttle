The ride

Motorway to Marrakech, then across the Atlas, still on the motorway, to Agadir. About 5 hr 50 min of driving.

A lunch stop near Marrakech breaks the journey nicely.

Sedan or van: same price

On this route the sedan and the van both cost 3,400 MAD.

For four or more, or with surfboards, take the van: up to 8 people and their luggage.

In Agadir

The bay, the marina and the seafront hotels: the driver drops you at the entrance.

Going to Taghazout Bay? Choose it in the list of destinations: the price shows straight away.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca to Agadir?

3,400 MAD per car, by sedan (1 to 3 passengers) or by van (up to 8 passengers). Fixed price, fuel and tolls included.

How long does it take?

About 5 hr 50 min for 466 km, not counting stops.

Can we stop in Marrakech?

Yes, for a break. To visit Marrakech for several hours on the way, message us and we will give you the price.

How do I cancel?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your booking reference. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
