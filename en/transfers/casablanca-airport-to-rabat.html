The ride

We go round Casablanca, then follow the coastal motorway to Rabat, via Mohammedia and Bouznika.

About 1 hr 40 min, a little more if traffic is heavy leaving Casablanca.

In Rabat

Agdal, Hassan, Hay Riad, Souissi, the embassy district, the medina: the driver drops you at the address you give.

A meeting at a ministry or embassy on arrival day? Leave some margin: flights can be late.

Train or car?

From the airport, the train usually means changing in Casablanca with your luggage, then a taxi in Rabat.

By car it is direct and door to door, timed to your flight, even late at night.

Good to know

  • Prefer Rabat-Salé airport? Choose “Salé” in the list of airports.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca airport to Rabat?

900 MAD per car by sedan (1 to 3 passengers) and 1,000 MAD by van (up to 8 passengers). Fixed price, tolls included. From 10 pm to 6 am: +20%.

How long does it take?

About 1 hr 40 min for 105 km.

Can you take me back to the airport at the end of my stay?

Yes. Tick “Return trip” when you book: the return costs the same as the outward ride.

Can I book a return trip?

Yes. The trip starts from Casablanca, and your driver comes back for you in Rabat on the day you choose to bring you back. A return trip costs twice the one-way price: choose “Return trip” in the booking form.

Where do I meet the driver at the airport?

In the arrivals hall, just after customs, holding a sign with your name. You get the driver’s name and WhatsApp number the day before.

My flight is delayed. What happens?

We track your flight with the number you give us. Your driver comes at the real landing time and waits 60 minutes at no charge.
