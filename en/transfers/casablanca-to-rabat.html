The ride

Coastal motorway via Mohammedia and Bouznika. About 1 hr 25 min.

Leave a little earlier at rush hour: getting out of Casablanca can be slow.

Who is it for?

  • Appointments at a ministry, embassy or consulate.
  • Business meetings in Hay Riad or Agdal.
  • Sightseeing: Hassan Tower, the Mohammed V Mausoleum and the Kasbah of the Udayas.

Train or car?

Trains between Casablanca and Rabat are frequent, about one hour from station to station.

The car is worth it for a group, with luggage, or when the meeting is far from the station. The driver can also wait and bring you back.

Good to know

  • Visit Rabat and come back in the evening? The “Rabat, the capital” trip (8 hours with a driver) leaves from Casablanca: see our day trips.
  • Same price from any part of Casablanca, and from Mohammedia.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Casablanca to Rabat?

900 MAD per car by sedan (1 to 3 passengers) and 1,000 MAD by van (up to 8 passengers). Fixed price, tolls included.

Can the driver wait during my appointment?

Yes. Choose “Chauffeur by the hour” (from 3 hours) in the booking form, or tick “Return trip”.

And from Rabat to Casablanca airport?

Yes, same price: 900 MAD by sedan, 1,000 MAD by van.

How do I cancel?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your booking reference. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
