Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Casablanca → Marrakech → Tichka pass → OuarzazateDay 1 · ≈ 450 km · ≈ 7 hr drive

    Early start from Casablanca. Break in Marrakech, then across the High Atlas over the Tichka pass (2,260 m). Visit the ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, then Ouarzazate. Night in Ouarzazate.

  2. Ouarzazate → Dades gorges → Todra gorges → MerzougaDay 2 · ≈ 380 km · ≈ 6.5 hr drive

    The road of the kasbahs through Skoura and the Dades valley, with its gorges. Stop at the Todra gorges. Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon: sunset over the dunes and a night in a desert camp.

  3. Merzouga → Midelt → Middle Atlas → FezDay 3 · ≈ 470 km · ≈ 7 hr drive

    Sunrise over Erg Chebbi. Drive north through the Ziz valley and Midelt, then the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas. Night in Fez.

  4. Fez → CasablancaDay 4 · ≈ 300 km · ≈ 3.5 hr drive

    Morning in Fez if you wish, then the motorway to Casablanca. Arrival in the afternoon.

Highlights of the tour

  • The Tichka pass: the road crosses the High Atlas at 2,260 m, with wide mountain views.
  • Ait Ben Haddou: a fortified earthen village, a UNESCO World Heritage site and the set of many films.
  • The Dades and Todra gorges: high red cliffs, palm groves and kasbahs.
  • Erg Chebbi: the great sand dunes of Merzouga, which change colour at sunrise and sunset.
  • The Middle Atlas: Midelt, then the cedar forests on the road to Fez.

A night in the desert

In Merzouga, camps welcome travellers for the night in tents among the dunes. You often ride there on a camel at sunset, and get up early to watch the sun rise over Erg Chebbi.

The camp is not included in the price: tell us its name when you book, and your driver drops you at the meeting point and picks you up the next morning.

When to go

Spring and autumn are the best seasons. In summer it is often over 40 °C in the afternoon in the desert. In winter the days are mild but the nights are cold, and the Tichka pass can have snow.

Good to know

  • Bring a small bag for the nights: the big suitcase stays in the car.
  • Pack a warm jacket: desert nights are cool, except in summer.
  • Your driver stops for photos, breaks and meals: tell them what you want to see.
  • Four or more? Take the van: up to 8 people, for one price per car.

Are four days enough?

Yes, but the driving days are long: about 1,600 km in total. For more time there, such as an extra night in the desert or in Fez, message us on WhatsApp and we give you the price before you leave.

No. The price covers the car and the driver. Erg Chebbi camps usually offer the night in a tent, dinner, breakfast and the camel ride together. Tell us the name of your camp and your driver drops you at the meeting point.

What is the best season?

Spring and autumn. In summer it is often over 40 °C in the afternoon. In winter the desert nights are cold and the Tichka pass can have snow.

Does the driver stay with us for the whole tour?

Yes. The same car and the same driver stay with you for the 4 days, and your driver waits while you visit.
