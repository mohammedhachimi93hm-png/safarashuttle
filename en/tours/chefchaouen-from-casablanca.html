What to see in Chefchaouen

  • The blue medina: lanes painted blue, steps and small squares at the foot of the Rif mountains.
  • Outa el Hammam square: the heart of town, with the ochre-walled kasbah and the Great Mosque.
  • Ras el Ma: the spring at the edge of the medina, cool even in summer.
  • The Spanish Mosque: about a 30-minute walk up the hill, for the best view of the blue town.
  • Crafts: wool blankets and weaving, soaps, goat cheese from the Rif.

On the way back: Rabat

On the way back, stop in the capital: the Hassan Tower and the Mohammed V Mausoleum, then the blue and white lanes of the Kasbah of the Udayas, facing the ocean.

Good to know

  • The medina is car-free: your driver drops you as close as possible. Bring a small bag for the night.
  • Wear good shoes: the lanes go up and down.
  • Bring a jacket: evenings are cool in the Rif, especially in winter.
  • Four or more? Take the van: up to 8 people, for one price per car.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Chefchaouen tour from Casablanca?

6,670 MAD per car for the 2 days by sedan (1 to 3 people), 7,540 MAD by van (up to 8 people) and 12,010 MAD by minibus (up to 17 people). It is the price of the whole car with driver, not a price per person. Fuel and tolls included.

Can I visit Chefchaouen as a day trip from Casablanca?

It can be done, but it is tiring: about 4 hours of driving each way for a few hours there. We suggest 2 days with one night in Chefchaouen. For a one-way ride, see our Casablanca → Chefchaouen transfer.

Where do we sleep in Chefchaouen?

In the riad or guesthouse of your choice. The night is not included in the price: tell us where you are staying when you book, and your driver drops you as close as possible.

How long is the drive?

About 4 hours from Casablanca to Chefchaouen (≈ 320 km), with the bends of the Rif at the end. The way back via Rabat is about 4.5 hours of driving, not counting visits.

Can we change the itinerary?

Yes: the Akchour waterfalls, Tetouan or an extra night, for example. Message us on WhatsApp and we give you the price before you leave.

How do I cancel?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your booking reference. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before departure.
