Frequently asked questions

How much is a private driver in Casablanca?

By sedan: 650 MAD for 3 hours, 1,350 MAD for 6 hours, 2,000 MAD for 9 hours. By van (up to 8 people): 1,000 MAD for 3 hours, 3,000 MAD for 9 hours.

What is the minimum time?

3 hours. After that you choose the number of hours, up to 12.

Does the driver wait between appointments?

Yes. The car and driver stay at your disposal for all the hours you book.

Can I book for several days?

Yes. For several days, see our tours: Chefchaouen in 2 days or the Merzouga desert in 4 days. For another itinerary, message us on WhatsApp.

How do I cancel?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your booking reference. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
