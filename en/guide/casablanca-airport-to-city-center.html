Mohammed V Airport → Casablanca city center: train, bus, taxi or private driver?

Four ways to reach the city, about 30 km away. The train and the Aérobus are the cheapest; a taxi or a private driver takes you to your door.

The train (ONCF)

The station is under Terminal 1 (level −1): follow the “Train” signs. The airport shuttle train leaves about once an hour for Casablanca and stops at Casa-Voyageurs, the main station, after about 30 minutes.

Fare: about 40–50 MAD in 2nd class, a little more in 1st. For Rabat or Marrakech, change trains at Casa-Voyageurs.

Note: since September 2026, works on the network have changed some times and stops. Check your train on oncf-voyages.ma before you travel. The last train usually leaves around 10–11 pm: if you land later, plan another option.

The Aérobus (bus to the center)

Since December 2025, the Aérobus, set up by the airport authority ONDA with Alsa, links Terminals 1 and 2 with Casa-Port station in the city center. It runs 24/7, about every 25 minutes by day and every 40 minutes at night.

Fare: 50 MAD per person, cash or card. It is the cheapest option at night, but you then need a taxi from Casa-Port to your hotel.

The grand taxi

The taxi rank is at Terminal 1 and works day and night. To central Casablanca, the fare is around 300 MAD per car by day (the rate posted by ONDA in recent years), and often goes up in the evening and at night. A grand taxi takes up to 6 passengers.

Our tips: agree on the price before you get in, carry cash in dirhams, and go straight to the official rank rather than following someone offering a “taxi” in the hall.

Uber and ride-hailing apps

Uber has been back in Casablanca since late 2025 (UberX and UberXL, with licensed transport companies). Careem and other apps also exist. Pick-up at the airport is not always possible: check in the app when you land and keep a backup plan, especially at night.

A private driver (our service)

Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. They track your flight, wait 60 minutes free if it is late, carry your bags and drop you at your address.

Fixed price per car: 350 MAD by sedan (1–3 people), 400 MAD by van (up to 8). 10 pm to 6 am: +20%. You see the price before you book; nothing is added later. See the route and all prices.

Arriving at night?

After the last train, you can take the Aérobus (24/7, 50 MAD to Casa-Port), a grand taxi (more expensive at night) or a driver booked in advance. With children, lots of luggage or a very late flight, booking ahead saves you waiting and haggling at the exit.

Good to know

  • The airport is about 30 km south of Casablanca: allow 30 to 60 minutes by car depending on the time of day.
  • Terminal 1 is home to Royal Air Maroc among others; the train station and the taxi rank are there.
  • Keep some dirhams for taxis and tips: there are exchange desks and cash machines at the airport.
  • Going to Rabat, Marrakech or El Jadida? A direct ride saves changing trains with your luggage.
  • A new terminal is being built until 2029: follow the signs carefully, some access routes may change.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Mohammed V Airport to central Casablanca?

Around 300 MAD per car by day, often more in the evening and at night. With a private driver booked ahead: 350 MAD by sedan, fixed price, met with a name sign (+20% from 10 pm to 6 am).

Is there a train from the airport to the city center?

Yes. The station is under Terminal 1. There is about one train an hour to Casa-Voyageurs (≈ 30 minutes), for about 40–50 MAD. There are no trains late at night: check the timetable on oncf-voyages.ma.

Is there a bus from the airport to Casablanca?

Yes, the Aérobus: 50 MAD per person, 24/7, between Terminals 1 and 2 and Casa-Port station in the center.

Does Uber work in Casablanca?

Yes, Uber came back to Casablanca in late 2025. At the airport, pick-up is not always possible: check in the app when you land.

How long does it take from the airport to the city center?

About 30 minutes by train to Casa-Voyageurs, and 30 to 60 minutes by car depending on traffic.

What if I arrive late in the evening?

Take the Aérobus (24/7) or a grand taxi, or book a driver in advance: they track your flight and wait for you, even if it is late.
